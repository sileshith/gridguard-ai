Interpretation. Pooled capture ranks all validation cases together. Mean scenario recall gives each positive scenario equal weight after ranking within that scenario. These are different review policies.

02 / METHOD, WITH GUARDRAILS

Keep related scenarios together.

Each observation pairs an operating scenario with a candidate outage. Splitting distinct scenario IDs first prevents related outage rows from crossing training, validation and test partitions.

  1. ValidateSchema, finite values, binary targets and scenario–outage integrity.
  2. Group & compareLR, RF and GBT. Metadata never enters the nine-feature vector.
  3. Choose & freezeTraining-only grouped search and validation analysis. Policy frozen. Final test unevaluated.
