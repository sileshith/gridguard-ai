# GridRail access workflow

GridRail AI has completed core manual validation in an ASU-managed ChatGPT workspace. Public GPT sharing is disabled by the workspace administrator. Only accounts recognized by that workspace can currently receive direct GPT access. External personal-email accounts did not resolve in the reported manual sharing check.

The public [request form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSddC199Z7tKwWrrFcPfUeZEiMqhM-7-hKzAlmLFpLpcesjSRA/viewform) accepts access requests and expressions of interest. An ASU email address alone does not guarantee workspace recognition or approval.

1. A visitor submits the existing form, with a contact email and optional separate workspace email.
2. The owner reviews the request. Notifications and the linked response spreadsheet support private request handling.
3. The owner checks the supplied workspace account in GridRail AI → Edit GPT → Share.
4. If the account resolves as an eligible workspace user, the owner may add it with **Can chat** permission and save.
5. Only after permission is granted may the owner send the restricted GPT link to the approved requester. The requester must sign in with the eligible workspace account.
6. If the account is not recognized, the owner explains the current workspace restriction without promising future access. The public dashboard, GitHub repository and portfolio remain available.
7. No form response automatically grants access. Requester details and the response sheet are not published.

The restricted GPT address and owner-use approval and external-request templates are retained separately from this public repository. Do not post the restricted link in public documentation, the dashboard, portfolio or social posts.
